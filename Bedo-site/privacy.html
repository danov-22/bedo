Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 20, 2026

bedo is an independent planning web app. This policy explains what information bedo uses, why it is used, where it is stored, and the choices available to you.

Information bedo uses

  • Google account information: when you sign in, bedo receives your Google account identifier and basic profile information such as your name, email address, and profile picture.
  • Planning content: schedules, time blocks, categories, completion status, brainstorm notes, preferences, and other content you create.
  • Google Calendar information: only if you choose to import it, bedo requests read-only access and copies supported timed events from your primary calendar into your bedo workspace. The import is optional and is not continuous two-way synchronization.
  • Technical information: local browser storage, sync state, and basic hosting logs that may include IP address, browser type, request time, and errors.

How Google user data is used

Google account data is used to authenticate you and identify your private workspace. Google Drive access is used only to create, read, and update bedo’s private app-data file in your own Google Drive. Google Calendar read-only access is used only when you request an import. bedo does not use Google user data for advertising, sell it, or use it to train artificial-intelligence models.

bedo’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Where information is stored

Your working copy and rolling autosave backups are stored locally in your browser. If you connect Google Drive, a copy is stored in the hidden app-data area of your own Google Drive account. bedo cannot browse your other Drive files through this permission. Live schedule links require the selected schedule data to be sent to bedo’s sharing service so the link can be viewed by others.

Sharing and disclosure

Your workspace is private by default. If you create a live sharing link, anyone who has that link can see the dates and schedule items included in the selected range. Brainstorm notes are not included. You can disable a sharing link from the app. Information may also be processed by service providers necessary to operate bedo, including Google and Vercel, under their own terms and privacy policies, or disclosed when required by law.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains on a device until you clear the site’s browser data. Google Drive app data remains in your Google account until it is deleted or access is removed. Shared schedule data remains until its link is disabled or the service data is removed. You may download your data from the app. To request deletion of server-held account or sharing data, contact the address below.

Your choices and security

You can use the disposable demo without signing in, decline Calendar import, disconnect Google access in your Google Account settings, disable sharing links, export your data, or stop using bedo. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. A note marked private is concealed in the interface and excluded from shared schedules; it is not separately end-to-end encrypted.

Children

bedo is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as bedo changes. Material revisions will be reflected by the date above. Questions, privacy requests, and deletion requests can be sent to dany10martin@gmail.com.
